# Why am I doing this?

If you are reading this, I wish you the very best. Really! You might say, that's nice of you. Really?  

I believe I have the medicine to your terminal illness. You might say, I'm not terminally ill.  
I would say, yes you are. Whether you die tomorrow or in 100 years, you will die.  

You might say, what is this medicine? What if I don't tell you? How evil of me!   
What if I do tell you? You might just call me evil...  

For this medicine I present might be hard to swallow. Your thoughts, speech, and actions would change.  
What if you don't want your thoughts, speech, and actions to change?   

What is this medicine?! The medicine is the Blood of Christ!! What??? Please, let me explain...  

*I am morally obligated to share my faith; hence, this repository is public. I intend on sharing and explaining my faith in this repository. If you are reading this, I wish you the very best.*
