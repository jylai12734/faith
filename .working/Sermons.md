## How a Thief Becomes a Philanthropist
- Ephesians 4:28 et al.
1. An Unregenerate Heart Might Prompt Stealing - Mark 7:21 ff.
Solution: Salvation - John 3:16; Acts 16:31; Rom. 6:23; 1 Cor. 15:3-4  
2. A Worldly Value System Might Prompt Stealing - Matt. 6:19-21; 1 Jn. 2:15-17
Solution: Establish Biblical priorities - 1 Tim. 6:9-11
3. Not Trusting God to Supply Your Needs Might Prompt Stealing
Solution: Pray, be financially wise, & trust God - Matt. 6:31, 31-33; Phil. 4:19  
4. Laziness Might Prompt Stealing  
Solution: Assume responsibility through hard work - Eph. 4:28b  
5. Greed and Selfishness Might Prompt Stealing
Solution: Look for opportunities to serve and give - Eph. 4:28c; II Thess. 3:10

## How to Live Between the Epiphanies
Epiphanies - the manifestation of Christ  
- We are called to clearly reject ungodliness
- We are called to be self-controlled
- Why? We have hope in eternal life
- Hope means wanting something
- Biblical hope means having assurance in God's promises
- Hope in eternal life means we are certain that God will grant us eternal life
- Our hope in eternal life dictates how we live.
## Let Us Love 1 John 3:18
The Greek word for love implies action rather than affection.  
- Biblical love involves good works and telling the truth rather than words of affection
